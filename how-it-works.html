How FarmEquip Works

A transparent, dispute-free rental workflow built around the needs of Indian farming communities.

1
DISCOVER & COMPARE

Find the Right Machinery Near Your Village

Enter your village name, mandal, or PIN code to view active tractors, harvesters, seeders, or spraying drones located within your radius. Filter by horsepower, billing rate (per acre, per hour, or per day), and operator availability.

2
BOOK & ESCROW HOLD

Reserve with Protected Security Deposit

Select your required dates and whether you need delivery or a trained operator. Our automated scheduling calendar prevents double-bookings. Pay the rental fee and a nominal refundable deposit via secure UPI or net banking. Funds remain safely in escrow until work is completed.

3
HANDOVER INSPECTION

Digital Photographic Handover

When machinery arrives at your field, the farmer and provider complete a 2-minute mobile photo inspection checklist. Hour meters, fuel level, and condition are logged to eliminate disputes before field work begins.

4
RETURN & INSTANT REFUND

Return Machinery & Receive Deposit Refund

Once your plowing, spraying, or harvesting is completed, mark the equipment for return. Both parties confirm condition, and the security deposit is credited back into your UPI account immediately.
